Tomato Study User Guide

A visual walk-through of the main application window, the comprehensive Settings panel, and keyboard-first shortcuts.

1. Main Interface Walkthrough

The primary viewport is split into three functional layers designed for zero visual noise.

Layer 1

Status Header

Displays the current cycle state (FOCUS WORK in ruby accent, SHORT BREAK, or LONG BREAK) along with current session tracking (e.g. SESSION 1 OF 4). In Flowtime, indicates current deep work state.

Layer 2

Clock Display Area

Renders either the signature split-flap 2.5D flip cards or clean digital typography. Shows minutes and seconds with automatic 3-digit hour overflow support for extended Flowtime blocks.

Layer 3

Floating Control Bar

Auto-dimming controls: Start/Pause button, Reset button, Take Break button (active in Flowtime), Settings cog, and expandable lo-fi volume slider with mute toggle. Automatically fades during active focus.

2. Settings Drawer Configuration

Press S or click the gear button to slide open the configuration drawer.

Preset & Duration Controls

  • Preset Dropdown: Select Pomodoro, Flowtime, 50/10 Focus, or Custom.
  • Durations: Set custom Work Duration (min), Short Break (min), and Long Break (min).
  • Long Break Interval: Choose how many cycles elapse before a long restorative break (default: 4).
  • Auto-Start Options: Check Auto-Start Breaks and Auto-Start Work to run continuously without manual clicks.

Visual & Audio Customization

  • Clock Theme: Toggle between mechanical Flip cards and minimal Digital font.
  • Accent Palette: Choose Ruby Red (#ff4d4d), Emerald (#2ecc71), Sky Blue (#3498db), Gold (#f1c40f), or Crisp White.
  • GPU Shaders: Adjust mouse trail delay, star drift speed, and meteor frequency.
  • Sound Options: Alarm volume slider, soft ticking sound toggle, and late.sh radio volume.
Digital Wellbeing & Security Settings

Endpoint & Privacy Controls

Inside the Wellbeing section of the Settings drawer, toggle Strict Focus Mode to block social distractions and lock window exits, Privacy Screen Lock to lock your workstation when stepping into break, and AES-256 Storage Encryption to protect your configuration files on disk.

3. Keyboard Shortcuts & Rebinding Guide

Control your focus timer entirely from the keyboard without breaking hand posture.

Default Key Action How to Rebind
Space Start / Pause Timer Click the keybind row in Settings, press any desired key, and save.
R Reset Current Cycle Settings > Keybinds > Reset Key
S Toggle Settings Panel Settings > Keybinds > Settings Key
Z Toggle Zen Mode Settings > Keybinds > Zen Mode Key (or double-click background)
P Toggle Lo-Fi Stream Settings > Keybinds > Stream Key
M Mute / Unmute Audio Settings > Keybinds > Mute Key
Pro Tip

How Rebinding Works in Settings

Open Settings with S, scroll down to KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS, click on the key button you wish to alter (it will highlight in red), press any key on your keyboard, and click SAVE & APPLY at the bottom. Your bindings are saved to encrypted local configuration and take effect immediately.
